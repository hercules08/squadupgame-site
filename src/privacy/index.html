Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Squad Up mobile game for Android and iOS and this website, operated by Swyft Studios LLC.

Effective October 4, 2026

Who can play

Squad Up is intended for teens ages 13 and older and adults. It is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information to us, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

Information involved in playing

You do not need a Squad Up account to play. The app uses motion-sensor readings on your device to recognize game gestures. The current app processes those readings for gameplay; it does not intentionally send a continuous raw accelerometer stream to our servers. Deck choices, scores, purchases, and settings may be stored locally on your device. Diagnostic or gameplay events may appear in device logs.

The app requests published deck information from our catalog service at admin.squadupgame.com. Like other internet services, the hosting provider may process connection information such as IP address, request time, and device or browser details to deliver and secure the service. The current catalog request does not require a player account.

Ads and in-app purchases

Squad Up may display ads provided by Google AdMob. Google’s mobile ads technology may collect and share information such as IP address, app interactions, diagnostics, and device or advertising identifiers for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. Available ad-consent choices depend on your region and device settings. You can also manage advertising preferences in your device settings. Learn more in Google’s advertising information.

Optional purchases, including decks or removing ads, are processed by Google Play or Apple’s App Store. We receive purchase status needed to unlock the item; we do not receive your full payment-card number. Purchase records are also governed by the store’s privacy policy.

Website and messages

This website does not intentionally load analytics or advertising scripts or set marketing cookies. Its hosting provider may process standard connection logs to serve and secure pages. If you email us for help, we use your email address and the information you send to respond and resolve the request. Please do not include payment-card details or other sensitive information in a support message.

How information is used and shared

We use information to operate the game and catalog, deliver purchases, respond to support requests, maintain security, and understand technical problems. We work with service providers involved in hosting, app distribution, billing, and advertising. Their processing is subject to their own policies and applicable agreements. We do not sell a player list or operate a player-account database for this app.

Retention, choices, and requests

Local game and purchase-state data generally remains on your device until you remove the app or clear its data; store purchase history is managed by the relevant store. Support messages and service logs are kept only as needed for support, security, legal obligations, and ordinary service operations. You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of information we control by emailing damola@swyftstudios.io. Tell us which app and device you used; we may need to verify your request. We cannot directly delete records held by Google, Apple, or another independent provider—use that provider’s privacy controls for those records.

Security and changes

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no internet service is completely secure. We may update this policy as the app or our providers change. The effective date above will change when we publish a material revision.

Contact

Swyft Studios LLC · damola@swyftstudios.io. For gameplay or purchase help, see our support page.
